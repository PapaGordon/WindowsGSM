Garry's Mod and other Source servers sometimes showed their console window after a restart, although Toggle Console was off. Pressing Toggle Console twice hid it again until the next start. It happened most often after the scheduled crontab restarts, but a normal start could hit it too.

The cause: SRCDS shows its console a second time shortly after it starts. If WindowsGSM had already hidden the window by then, it became visible again, and WindowsGSM never checked again. Now WindowsGSM keeps the window in line with Toggle Console for as long as the server runs, so it stays hidden or visible the way you set it.

To update, close WindowsGSM, keep a copy of the old `WindowsGSM.exe` and replace it with the new one, or use the ZIP. Your servers and saved credentials stay as they are.

Tested: Release build, 28 Source console checks including new checks for a console that shows itself again, 115 DayZ console checks and 188 built-in server checks. We also checked it on our four live Garry's Mod servers after the update.
